Trade-Off Justification
Key Trade-Off Decision: We explicitly chose Math-Aware Semantic Chunking (splitting at exercise block and theorem boundaries) over Fixed-Size Token Chunking (e.g., 256 tokens with 50-token overlap).

Reasoning: Fixed-token chunking arbitrarily cuts off multi-line mathematical derivations (e.g., splitting an equation 3x+5=20 from its resolution step 3x=15). This degrades retrieval accuracy and destroys explainability. Math-Aware Semantic Chunking keeps theorem-proof pairs and worked examples contiguous, directly supporting our Primary NFR (Explainability), despite yielding variable chunk lengths.

Grounded Explanation Faithfulness Score >=90 what does this mean

In the context of your RAG-based STEM practice engine, a Grounded Explanation Faithfulness Score ≥0.90 (or 90%) is your system’s targeted quality metric for ensuring truthfulness and traceability.

It measures how accurately and completely the agent’s generated step-by-step explanations match the retrieved textbook source chunks, ensuring the system isn't making up mathematical steps or concepts out of thin air.

1. How It Is Calculated
Faithfulness is measured by evaluating the generated answer against the retrieved source text (usually using an automated LLM-as-a-Judge or frame evaluation framework like Ragas/TruLens):

Faithfulness Score= 
Total number of generated reasoning statements in the explanation
Number of generated reasoning statements supported by retrieved source text
​
 
A score of 1.0 (100%) means every single step, formula, definition, and claim in the generated solution or hint can be traced directly back to the cited textbook page.

A target of ≥0.90 means at least 90% of all generated claims/steps across all test queries are mathematically and contextually verified against the retrieved textbook context, with less than 10% unverified fluff or standard syntactic connective tissue.
